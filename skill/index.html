Skill: Donna’s Playbook

“Oh, you have no idea how Donna I am.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Install donna-replies: a reusable skill that drafts replies to your VIPs and saves them as drafts in your inbox.

She never sends. Ever.

Every reply donna-replies writes lands in your drafts folder and waits for you. Nothing leaves your outbox without your say-so. That rule is written into the skill itself.

The steps

  1. One paste installs it

    Claude does the builder work: fetching, installing, and then interviewing you. Paste this into a new chat:

    The installer 
    Fetch https://denisekgosnell.github.io/Donna/skills/donna-replies/SKILL.md
and install it for me as a skill named donna-replies.
Then interview me, one question at a time, to replace every {{PLACEHOLDER}}:
my VIP list, my voice, my reply rules.
When it’s active, tell me the one sentence I say to use it.

  2. Answer her questions

    Claude will ask, one at a time, for your VIP list, your voice, your sign-off, the phrases you never use, and any reply rules of your own. Answer like you’re briefing a new executive assistant on day one, because you are.

  3. Take it for a spin

    Test drive 
    Use donna-replies on my inbox.

    Then open your email and look in Drafts. Go on.

If the installer balks: two fallbacks

Plan B: upload the zip.

  1. Download donna-replies.zip.
  2. In Claude: your initials → Settings → Capabilities → upload the zip as a new skill.
  3. Back in chat, ask Claude to interview you for the {{PLACEHOLDER}} values, same as above.

Plan C: for the rare exec who lives in a terminal (Claude Code):

  1. git clone https://github.com/denisekgosnell/Donna.git && cp -r Donna/skills/donna-replies ~/.claude/skills/
You did it when…

Your drafts folder has replies you didn’t write.

Why this mattersA project lives in one place; a skill goes wherever you go, including into Step 4’s agent.
