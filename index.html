The short version

Executives do not scale by making more decisions. They scale by writing down how decisions get made, then letting other people run the document. That is what a press release and FAQ is at Amazon. That is what a six-page narrative is. Today you will run the same flywheel with one substitution: the document is a skill file, and the thing reading it is an agent instead of a room.

It also explains the risk. AI does not fix a broken process. It runs it faster. If the document is vague, you have hired something that will execute vagueness perfectly, all day. So today, she interviews you, and the document writes itself around who you are.

What you’ll leave with

A working AI executive assistant that knows which executive you are, triages your inbox around who gets you and when, drafts replies in your voice (and never sends without you), and reports for duty every afternoon: a calendar invite in your inbox with the day’s triage inside, exactly when you want it.

House rules

Donna never sends an email and never deletes one. Everything she writes waits in your Drafts for your click. You are, and remain, the send button.

We build in waves: every step ends at a checkpoint where we regroup, so nobody gets left behind and nobody has to wait alone.

Four ways to use today

You will recognize yourself in one of these in Step 02, and Donna will run your setup differently for each. All four are correct; they change what to optimize for in the room.

  • Accuracy She confirms every rule back to you before locking it, and you can tighten any of it later. You leave with a playbook precise enough to defend in a review, plus a follow-up rule so work ships.
  • Time Shortest path in the room: smart defaults, fragments accepted, zero explanations. You leave with a running agent today, and a guardrail that keeps your people in the loop at your speed.
  • Trust Your setup is built around your people: who hears from you when, and which teams stay CC’d on what. You leave with drafts your people would believe you wrote, delivered on a deadline.
  • Vision Your setup aims at moving work forward: initiatives on watch, loops closed end to end. You leave with a repeatable method, and every draft fact-checked before it reaches you.
