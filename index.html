The loop

Open loops make more work. Closed loops make better work. AI makes open loops cheap. That is the whole problem.

Donna is a document: her playbook. Connect it, teach it, run it on a schedule, and on Fridays she proposes edits to it. You are not retraining Claude. You are retraining Donna.

  • Claude on your laptop. The Claude Desktop app for Mac or Windows, from claude.ai/download, signed in. A phone can follow along. The building needs the desktop app.
  • Free or paid? Steps 1 and 2 work on Free. Steps 3 and 4 put her on a schedule, and scheduled tasks need Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise. On Free you build the same agent and press start yourself. Steps 3 and 4 have a box with the exact words.
  • Your Google login handy. Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive. We connect them live. Head start: in Claude, Customize → Connectors, connect all three tonight.

  • One switch on. Click your initials, then Settings → Capabilities, and turn on Code execution and file creation. You never see code. The switch lets Claude open the playbook file she writes in Step 3.
