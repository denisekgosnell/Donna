01

First Impressions

“Today’s your lucky day. … Because it’s the day you get to meet Donna.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Give her a desk, connect your inbox, and get your first draft.

In this step
  1. Create her project.
  2. Connect Gmail, Calendar, and Drive.
  3. Two prompts: the question, then the first draft.
The steps

  1. Create her project

    In Claude’s left sidebar: Projects → New project. Name it Donna.

  2. Connect Gmail, Calendar, and Drive

    Left sidebar: Customize → Connectors.

    • Next to Gmail, click Connect. Sign in with your busiest inbox. Click Continue.
    • Do the same for Google Calendar and Google Drive.
    • All three say Connected. Done.

    Google’s screen may say the connector can send or delete. Her playbook forbids both.

  3. Ask the question

    Projects → Donna → New chat. Paste:

    Prompt 1 · the question 
    What landed in my inbox in the last 24 hours that actually needs ME? Top 5, one line each.

    It worked when she answers with five lines from your real inbox.

  4. Get your first draft

    Same chat. Paste:

    Prompt 2 · the first draft 
    Draft a reply to #1. Under 100 words, warm but decisive. Read my sent emails and learn how I write. Never use em dashes. Never use AI speech patterns. Save it as a draft in my email so I can review it there. Do not send it.

    Open Gmail → Drafts. There it is. Type ✓ in the Zoom chat.

If something goes wrong
  • Connected, but she can’t see your mail? Quit Claude completely and open it again.
  • “Administrator hasn’t approved this”? Your work account is locked down. Use a personal Gmail today.
  • Still stuck? Say so in the Zoom chat or raise your hand. A TA takes you into a Zoom room, 1:1.
Or paste this into Claude 
Walk me through connecting my Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive to Claude. Check Anthropic’s current help docs first so the steps match my screen today. Give me numbered steps with the exact buttons, and what to do if a connector is missing or says the administrator hasn’t approved it.
You did it when…

A draft you didn’t write is waiting in your drafts folder.
