Build Your Own Donna All steps

Step 01 · ~15 min

01

First Impressions

“Today’s your lucky day. … Because it’s the day you get to meet Donna.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Give her a desk, connect your inbox, and watch her read it. You leave this step with a draft you didn’t write.

The steps

  1. Create her project. The desk before the mail.

    In Claude’s sidebar: Projects → New project. Name it Donna.

    Everything you do today happens inside this project. It’s where she keeps what she learns about you.

  2. Connect your email, calendar, and files

    Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then Settings → Connectors. Denise switched these on for the whole workspace; all you do is click Connect.

    • In the connectors list, find Gmail and click Connect.
    • Your browser opens a Google sign-in page. Choose your work account.
    • Google lists what Claude will be able to see. Click Continue (some screens say Allow).
    • Do the exact same for Google Calendar and Google Drive. All three, now: Donna needs the calendar for Step 3’s finale.
    • Back in Claude, each one reads Connected. That’s it.
    • In the connectors list, find Microsoft 365 and click Connect.
    • Your browser opens a Microsoft sign-in page. Enter your work email, then your password (and the usual MFA tap, if your company uses one).
    • Microsoft lists the permissions. Click Accept.
    • Good news: one connection covers Outlook mail, your calendar, and OneDrive. Everything Donna needs today, including Step 3’s finale.
    • Back in Claude, it reads Connected. That’s it.
    Stuck connecting? Paste this
    The rescue prompt 
    Walk me through connecting my work email and calendar to Claude. First ask whether I’m on Gmail or Outlook, Mac or Windows, and whether IT manages my account, and check Anthropic’s current help docs before answering so the steps match what’s actually on my screen today.
Then give me numbered steps naming the exact buttons to click, a way to verify it worked, and what to do if the connector isn’t listed or I hit an “administrator hasn’t approved this” error.

  3. Ask the question you’d ask an assistant

    Open your Donna project, start a new chat, and paste:

    Prompt 1 · the question 
    What landed in my inbox in the last 24 hours that actually needs ME?
Top 5, one line each.

    You’re on track when she answers with five lines from your actual inbox. This is the moment most rooms go quiet.

  4. Get your first draft

    Same chat, one more paste:

    Prompt 2 · the first draft 
    Draft a reply to #1. Under 100 words, warm but decisive.
Save it as a draft in my email so I can review it there.
Do not send it.

    Now switch to your email and open your Drafts folder. Go on.

You did it when…

A draft you didn’t write is waiting in your drafts folder.
