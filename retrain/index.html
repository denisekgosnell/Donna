04

Her Performance Review

“I know about everybody.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Every Friday she reads her week against yours and proposes changes to her playbook. Monday, you approve. That is the loop, closed.

In this step
  1. Schedule the Friday retrain.
  2. Watch her first review land.
  3. Monday: sign it.
The steps

  1. Give her the Friday shift

    Same place as the 4:30 shift: Scheduled → New task → Create with Claude. Paste:

    The weekly order 
    Every Friday at 3:00 PM my local time, use my donna-triage skill to retrain:
1. Read this week: the triage reports you wrote, the drafts you saved, and the emails I actually sent.
2. Compare. Who did I answer fast that is not on my Reply-Now list? Which threads did I spend my week on that are not in my priorities? Where did my sent emails sound different from your drafts?
3. Propose changes to my playbook: people, priorities, voice. Keep the hard rules exactly as they are: never send, never delete.
4. Write “Donna’s Week in Review” into a Google Calendar event, Monday 8:00 to 8:05 AM, proposed changes at the top, with an EMAIL reminder 1 minute before.
5. Save the proposed changes as a document called “Donna’s Week in Review” in a Google Drive folder called “Donna”.
Also do a dry run right now on this week so far, and for the dry run put the event on today’s calendar 10 minutes from now instead of Monday.

    The last line runs it right now, on this week so far. Your Sent folder since Monday gives her plenty to say.

    Free plan? You press start.

    Every Friday at 3:00, open your Donna project, start a new chat, and paste this. Set a phone reminder. Paste it now for today’s dry run.

    The Friday retrain, started by you 
    Use my donna-triage skill to retrain on this week so far. Read the triage reports you wrote, the drafts you saved, and the emails I actually sent. Compare them and propose changes to my playbook: people, priorities, voice. Keep the hard rules exactly as they are. Write “Donna’s Week in Review” into a Google Calendar event today, 10 minutes from now, with the proposed changes at the top and an EMAIL reminder 1 minute before, and save it as a document called “Donna’s Week in Review” in a Google Drive folder called “Donna”.

  2. Watch her first review land

    • Donna’s Week in Review appears on today’s calendar, about ten minutes out.
    • Open it. Her proposed changes are at the top, numbered, each with a because.
    • One minute before it starts, the email arrives. Type ✓ in the Zoom chat.

    This is 1:20 PM ET (10:20 AM PT · 6:20 PM London · 7:20 PM Paris). Four stations lit.

  3. Monday: sign it

    Read the review. If you agree, open your Donna project and paste:

    The signature 
    Approve this week’s review. It is the document “Donna’s Week in Review” in my Google Drive folder “Donna”. Update my donna-triage skill with the approved changes, right here in this chat, so I can save it with one click.

    Click Save (or Update) on the skill card, as in Step 3. If a second donna-triage appears under Customize → Skills, delete the older one.

    “Approve 1 and 3” or “Reject 2” work too. Nothing changes until you say so. Her hard rules never change at all.

What just happened

Connect, teach, run, retrain. Anything that learns from data has run this loop for over fifty years. You are not retraining Claude. You are retraining Donna, and Donna is a document you own. If she gets it wrong, you do not need a better prompt. You need to retrain with better info, and more specific info.

You did it when…

A performance review you didn’t write is on your calendar, with the changes she wants to make at the top.

Why this mattersEveryone else is still telling AI what to do. You built the Friday where she tells you what she got wrong.
