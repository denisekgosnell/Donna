03

Donna, Full Time

“If you were ever lucky enough to have me, you wouldn’t want to share.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

The playbook leaves your hands and goes on the clock: every weekday she triages your inbox, then a calendar invite lands in your email with the whole triage inside. No new app to check. Your calendar reports to you.

She drafts. You send.

Scheduled or not, every reply she writes waits in your drafts folder for your say-so, and she never deletes a thing. The one send you grant her: the RSVP when she accepts a meeting invite onto a free calendar. It’s written into the skill itself: never send a reply, never delete anything.

The steps

  1. She writes her own playbook file

    Stay in your Step 2 chat (the one with your first triage table; it holds everything she learned about you) and paste:

    Paste in the same chat 
    Turn this conversation’s triage setup into a reusable skill file named donna-triage.
Fill in the template we used with everything you learned about me: my executive type, my tiers and lists, my voice, and my final-question guardrail. Keep the Setup Interview and the scheduled-task sections so the skill still knows how to reconfigure and how to run on a schedule.
Keep the hard rules intact: never send a reply, never delete anything, meeting-invite RSVPs only on a free calendar.
Then give me the finished SKILL.md as a file I can download.

    You’re on track when a file card appears in the chat with a download arrow on it.

  2. Download it, then upload it to your skills

    Three moves, watch the screen share:

    • Click the file card’s Download arrow. It saves as SKILL.md.
    • Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then Settings → Skills → Upload skill, and pick the file from your computer.
    • Confirm it: donna-triage now shows in your skills list.
    Donna’s note The only thing anyone forgets here is where the file went. Unless you chose a spot, it’s in your Downloads folder, and it’s the newest file there, sitting right at the top.

    That skills-list entry is the confirmation for this step. Nobody touches the schedule until it’s there.

  3. Give her the 3:42 shift (the automation)

    From Home: Scheduled tasks → New task. Paste the standing order:

    The standing order 
    Every weekday at 3:42 PM Eastern, use my donna-triage skill:
1. Triage today’s inbox. Save the Reply-Now drafts to my email Drafts.
2. Create a Google Calendar event today, starting 10 minutes after this
   task starts and 5 minutes long, titled “Inbox Triage: Donna”.
3. Write the whole triage report INTO the event description.
   Short lines, tier-name headers, one line per thread:
   sender, subject, next move. No tables. Easy to read on a phone.
4. Set an EMAIL reminder on the event, 1 minute before.
If today’s 3:42 run has already passed, also do a dry run right now
so I can watch the first one land.
    Donna’s note Take yourself off the approval loop so the shift runs hands-free: in the chat’s lower left, click Accept edits, choose Bypass permissions, then click Bypass permissions to confirm. That switch is Claude asking how much she may do without checking in first. Her hard rules still stand either way: drafts only, never delete.

    Google Calendar sends email reminders natively. Every weekday around 3:51 PM, the triage lands on top of the very inbox it triaged, from a sender you already trust: your own calendar.

  4. Watch the first shift land

    Set the task before 3:42 and it fires live in this room; land here later and the last line makes her work right now. Either way, three confirmations within a few minutes:

    • Inbox Triage: Donna sits on today’s calendar, about ten minutes out.
    • Open the event: your whole triage is in the description, tier by tier.
    • One minute before it starts, the email arrives. Read your inbox’s summary, in your inbox, without opening a single thread.

    While you wait: check your Drafts folder. The Reply-Now drafts from this run are already sitting there.

  5. If time allows: the Friday wrap (automation #2)

    Same pattern, weekly cadence, for the slower tiers. Scheduled tasks → New task:

    The weekly order · optional 
    Every Friday at 3:00 PM, use my donna-triage skill on the past week:
On Watch movement, Weekly Batch threads, and anything still
waiting on me. Create a calendar event Monday 8:00–8:05 AM titled
“Week Ahead: Donna” with the report in the event description
and an email reminder 1 minute before.

    If you skipped the optional tiers in Step 2, set this up after the workshop: tell her “set up my watch tier” first.

The trick

Claude never emails you. Your calendar does. The triage arrives one minute before its slot, from the one sender that never lands in spam: you. No new tool, no new noise. One appointment a day with everything that matters inside it.

The last thing: she grows with you

Your VIPs will change. Your #1 deal will close. When they do, update her in four moves; this loop is the whole game:

1 · Come back to your Donna project chat any time and tell her what changed: “Move Sam to Reply Now,” or just “reconfigure.”
2 · Say “write my skill file again” and download the fresh SKILL.md.
3 · Back in Settings → Skills, replace donna-triage with the new file.
4 · That’s it. The 3:42 shift runs the new rules the same afternoon. Teach her once, update her in minutes, never re-explain.

You did it when…

A calendar invite from your own calendar lands in your inbox with the day’s triage written inside it.

Why this mattersYou didn’t just use an assistant today. You hired one, wrote her playbook, and put her on payroll.
