Donna, Full Time

“Oh, you have no idea how Donna I am.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Her playbook leaves the chat and goes on the clock. Every weekday she triages your inbox, and an email from your own calendar brings you the result.

In this step
  1. She writes her playbook file.
  2. You upload it as a skill.
  3. Schedule the 4:30 shift.

Station 3 of 4: Run. The work gets done, on a schedule.

She drafts. You send.

Every reply she writes waits in your drafts folder for your say-so, and she never deletes a thing. It is written into the skill itself.

The steps

  1. She writes her own playbook file

    Stay in your Step 2 chat (it holds everything she learned about you) and paste:

    Paste in the same chat 
    Turn this conversation’s triage setup into a reusable skill named donna-triage. Fill in the template we used with everything you learned about me: my people and tiers, my priorities, my voice. Keep the daily shift, the Friday retrain, and the Setup Interview sections so the skill knows how to run on a schedule, how to retrain, and how to reconfigure. Keep the hard rules exactly as written: never send, never delete. Then give me the finished skill as a downloadable zip named donna-triage.zip containing donna-triage/SKILL.md.

    You’re on track when a file card with a download arrow appears in the chat.

  2. Download it, then upload it to your skills

    Three moves, watch the screen share:

    • Click the file card’s Download arrow. It saves as donna-triage.zip, in your Downloads folder.
    • Left sidebar: Customize → Skills → + → Create skill → Upload a skill. In the file window, open Downloads, pick donna-triage.zip (newest, at the top), then Open.
    • Confirm: donna-triage now shows in your skills list.
    Donna’s note · the gotchas

    No Skills under Customize? The switch is off. Your initials → Settings → Capabilities → turn on Code execution and file creation, then look again.

    Upload refused? The zip must hold a folder named donna-triage with SKILL.md inside. If she gave you a loose file, ask her: “Give me that as donna-triage.zip containing donna-triage/SKILL.md.”

    Uploaded, but she ignores it? Name it. Say “use my donna-triage skill.” The standing order below already does.

    The workaround, on every plan and every build: skip the upload. Ask her “Print the finished playbook as plain text,” copy it, and paste it at the top of the standing order in the next step, above the line that starts “Every weekday at 4:30 PM my local time.” The task carries her playbook inside it.

    Can’t find Skills or Scheduled tasks? Paste this
    The rescue prompt 
    In the version of the Claude desktop app I’m running right now, where do I (1) upload a skill zip and (2) create a scheduled task? Name the exact menu items and buttons, in order, and tell me how to confirm each worked.

    The skills-list entry is the confirmation. Type ✓ in the Zoom chat when you see it. Still stuck? Say so in the chat or raise your hand, and a TA takes you into a Zoom room, 1:1.

  3. Give her the 4:30 shift (the automation)

    That is 4:30 PM (16:30) in your own time zone. Left sidebar: Scheduled → New task → Create with Claude. Paste the standing order:

    The standing order 
    Every weekday at 4:30 PM my local time, use my donna-triage skill:
1. Triage today’s inbox. Save the Reply-Now drafts to my email Drafts.
2. Create a Google Calendar event today, starting 10 minutes after this task starts and 5 minutes long, titled “Inbox Triage: Donna”.
3. Write the whole triage report INTO the event description. Short lines, tier-name headers, one line per thread: sender, subject, next move. No tables. Easy to read on a phone.
4. Set an EMAIL reminder on the event, 1 minute before.
Also do a dry run right now so I can watch the first one land.
    Donna’s note Scheduled tasks run on Claude’s side, not on your laptop. Close the laptop; she still works at 4:30.

    If Claude asks which mode the task runs in, choose Auto so it runs hands-free. Her playbook, which you can read, is what stops her from doing more. Read her first few runs.

    Free plan? You press start.

    No Scheduled in your sidebar? Scheduled tasks are Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise. Everything else in this step is yours: the skill is built and uploaded. The only difference is who starts her shift. On Free, you do: at 4:30, open your Donna project, start a new chat, and paste this. Set a phone reminder for 4:30 on weekdays. That reminder is your scheduler.

    The 4:30 shift, started by you 
    Use my donna-triage skill. Triage today’s inbox and save the Reply-Now drafts to my email Drafts. Then create a Google Calendar event today, 10 minutes from now, 5 minutes long, titled “Inbox Triage: Donna”, with the whole report in the description and an EMAIL reminder 1 minute before.

    Paste it now for your dry run, and the checkpoint below is yours too. An agent you start by hand is still an agent.

    No Google calendar? She saves the same report as a draft email titled “Inbox Triage: Donna.” With Google Calendar, every weekday around 4:39 PM (16:39) the calendar emails you the triage, from a sender you already trust.

  4. Watch the first shift land

    The last line of the standing order makes her work right now. Three confirmations within a few minutes:

    • Inbox Triage: Donna sits on today’s calendar, about ten minutes out.
    • Open the event: your whole triage is in the description.
    • One minute before it starts, the email arrives.

    While you wait, check Drafts: the Reply-Now drafts are already there. When the event appears, type ✓ in the Zoom chat.

The trick

Claude never emails you. Your calendar does. The triage arrives from the one sender that never lands in spam: you.
