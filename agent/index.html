Donna, Full Time

“Oh, you have no idea how Donna I am.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Her playbook leaves the chat and goes on the clock: every weekday she triages your inbox, then an email from your own calendar lands in your inbox with the whole triage inside. No new app to check. Your calendar reports to you.

Station 3 of 4: Run. The work gets done, on a schedule.

She drafts. You send.

Scheduled or not, every reply she writes waits in your drafts folder for your say-so, and she never deletes a thing. It’s written into the skill itself: never send anything, never delete anything.

The steps

  1. She writes her own playbook file

    Stay in your Step 2 chat (the one with your first triage table; it holds everything she learned about you) and paste:

    Paste in the same chat 
    Turn this conversation’s triage setup into a reusable skill named donna-triage. Fill in the template we used with everything you learned about me: my people and tiers, my priorities, my voice. Keep the daily shift, the Friday retrain, and the Setup Interview sections so the skill knows how to run on a schedule, how to retrain, and how to reconfigure. Keep the hard rules exactly as written: never send, never delete. Then give me the finished skill as a downloadable zip named donna-triage.zip containing donna-triage/SKILL.md.

    You’re on track when a file card appears in the chat with a download arrow on it.

  2. Download it, then upload it to your skills

    Three moves, watch the screen share:

    • Click the file card’s Download arrow. It saves as donna-triage.zip.
    • In the left sidebar, open Customize, then Skills. Click +, then Create skill, then Upload a skill. A file window opens: click Downloads, click donna-triage.zip (newest, at the top), then Open.
    • Confirm it: donna-triage now shows in your skills list.
    Donna’s note The only thing anyone forgets here is where the file went. Unless you chose a spot, it’s in your Downloads folder, and it’s the newest file there, sitting right at the top. If your version of the app keeps Skills somewhere else (older builds: your initials → Settings → Capabilities), the rescue prompt below finds it.
    Can’t find Skills or Scheduled tasks? Paste this
    The rescue prompt 
    In the version of the Claude desktop app I’m running right now, where do I (1) upload a skill zip and (2) create a scheduled task? Name the exact menu items and buttons, in order, and tell me how to confirm each worked.

    That skills-list entry is the confirmation for this step. Nobody touches the schedule until it’s there. Type ✓ in the Zoom chat when you see it.

  3. Give her the 4:30 shift (the automation)

    That is 4:30 PM (16:30) in your own time zone. In the left sidebar: Scheduled → New task → Create with Claude. Paste the standing order:

    The standing order 
    Every weekday at 4:30 PM my local time, use my donna-triage skill:
1. Triage today’s inbox. Save the Reply-Now drafts to my email Drafts.
2. Create a Google Calendar event today, starting 10 minutes after this task starts and 5 minutes long, titled “Inbox Triage: Donna”.
3. Write the whole triage report INTO the event description. Short lines, tier-name headers, one line per thread: sender, subject, next move. No tables. Easy to read on a phone.
4. Set an EMAIL reminder on the event, 1 minute before.
Also do a dry run right now so I can watch the first one land.
    Donna’s note Scheduled tasks run on Claude’s side, not on your laptop. Close the laptop; she still works at 4:30.

    If Claude asks which mode the task should run in, choose Auto so it runs hands-free. Auto means she will not stop to ask before saving a draft or creating an event. The connector could do more; her playbook, which you can read, is what stops her. Read her first few runs.

    No Google calendar? Her playbook has a fallback: she saves the same report as a draft email titled “Inbox Triage: Donna.” Open Drafts at 4:40. With Google Calendar, every weekday around 4:39 PM (16:39) the calendar emails you the triage, on top of the very inbox it triaged, from a sender you already trust.

  4. Watch the first shift land

    The last line of the standing order makes her work right now. Three confirmations within a few minutes:

    • Inbox Triage: Donna sits on today’s calendar, about ten minutes out.
    • Open the event: your whole triage is in the description, tier by tier.
    • One minute before it starts, the email arrives. Read your inbox’s summary, in your inbox, without opening a single thread.

    While you wait: check your Drafts folder. The Reply-Now drafts from this run are already sitting there. When the event appears on your calendar, type ✓ in the Zoom chat.

The trick

Claude never emails you. Your calendar does. The triage arrives one minute before its slot, from the one sender that never lands in spam: you. No new tool, no new noise. One appointment a day with everything that matters inside it.
