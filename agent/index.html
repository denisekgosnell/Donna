Donna, Full Time

“Oh, you have no idea how Donna I am.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Her playbook becomes a file she keeps, and she works your inbox every weekday at 4:30. Your calendar emails you the result.

In this step
  1. She writes her playbook file.
  2. You upload it as a skill.
  3. Schedule the 4:30 shift.

She drafts. You send.

Every reply waits in your drafts folder. She never sends and never deletes. It is written into the skill.

The steps

  1. She writes her playbook file

    In your Step 2 chat, paste:

    Paste in the same chat 
    Turn this conversation’s triage setup into a reusable skill named donna-triage. Fill in the template we used with everything you learned about me: my people and tiers, my priorities, my voice. Keep the daily shift, the Friday retrain, and the Setup Interview sections so the skill knows how to run on a schedule, how to retrain, and how to reconfigure. Keep the hard rules exactly as written: never send, never delete. Then give me the finished skill as a downloadable zip named donna-triage.zip containing donna-triage/SKILL.md.

    It worked when a file card with a download arrow appears.

  2. Download it, then upload it as a skill

    • Click the file card’s Download arrow. It lands in Downloads as donna-triage.zip.
    • Left sidebar: Customize → Skills → + → Create skill → Upload a skill. Pick donna-triage.zip from Downloads.
    • donna-triage now shows in your skills list. Type ✓ in the Zoom chat.

  3. Give her the 4:30 shift

    Left sidebar: Scheduled → New task → Create with Claude. Paste:

    The standing order 
    Every weekday at 4:30 PM my local time, use my donna-triage skill:
1. Triage today’s inbox. Save the Reply-Now drafts to my email Drafts.
2. Create a Google Calendar event today, starting 10 minutes after this task starts and 5 minutes long, titled “Inbox Triage: Donna”.
3. Write the whole triage report INTO the event description. Short lines, tier-name headers, one line per thread: sender, subject, next move. No tables. Easy to read on a phone.
4. Set an EMAIL reminder on the event, 1 minute before.
Also do a dry run right now so I can watch the first one land.

    If Claude asks which mode, choose Auto. The task runs on Claude’s side, even with your laptop closed.

    Free plan? You press start.

    No Scheduled in your sidebar? Scheduled tasks are paid-plan only. Everything else is yours. At 4:30, open your Donna project, start a new chat, and paste this. Set a phone reminder for 4:30 on weekdays. That is your scheduler.

    The 4:30 shift, started by you 
    Use my donna-triage skill. Triage today’s inbox and save the Reply-Now drafts to my email Drafts. Then create a Google Calendar event today, 10 minutes from now, 5 minutes long, titled “Inbox Triage: Donna”, with the whole report in the description and an EMAIL reminder 1 minute before.

    Paste it now for your dry run. An agent you start by hand is still an agent.

  4. Watch the first one land

    • In a few minutes, Inbox Triage: Donna appears on today’s calendar, about ten minutes out.
    • Open it. The whole triage is in the description.
    • One minute before it starts, the email arrives. Type ✓ in the Zoom chat.

    While you wait, check Drafts. The Reply-Now drafts are already there.

If something goes wrong
  • No Skills under Customize? Click your initials → Settings → Capabilities and turn on Code execution and file creation.
  • Upload refused? The zip must hold a folder named donna-triage with SKILL.md inside. Ask her: “Give me that as donna-triage.zip containing donna-triage/SKILL.md.”
  • She ignores the skill? Name it: “use my donna-triage skill.”
  • Nothing uploads at all? Skip the skill. Ask her “Print the finished playbook as plain text,” copy it, and paste it at the top of the standing order above the line that starts “Every weekday at 4:30 PM my local time.”
  • No Google Calendar? She saves the same report as a draft email titled “Inbox Triage: Donna.”
  • Still stuck? Say so in the Zoom chat or raise your hand. A TA takes you into a Zoom room, 1:1.
