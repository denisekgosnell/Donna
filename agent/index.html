04

Agent: Donna, Full Time

“If you were ever lucky enough to have me, you wouldn’t want to share.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Donna works the evening shift: every weekday she clears your VIP replies, writes your briefing, and gets it onto your calendar. The notification arrives one minute before, with the doc link. That notification is the only email you now need.

The steps

  1. Open scheduled tasks

    In the Claude desktop app, click your initials → Settings → Scheduled tasks → New task.

    Don’t see it? Wave Denise over; it’s switched on for the whole workspace.

  2. Give Donna the evening shift

    Paste this as the task:

    The standing order 
    Every weekday at 4:30 PM:
1. Run my donna-replies skill on today’s inbox.
2. Write my Daily Briefing as a document titled "Daily Briefing: [today’s date]":
   • the 5 things in my inbox that need ME (one line + recommended action each)
   • the replies you drafted today
   • what you ignored and why, in one short paragraph
   • my first meeting tomorrow
3. Save it to my Google Drive in a folder called "Donna Briefings".
4. Create a calendar event today, 4:55–5:00 PM, called "📋 Briefing with Donna",
   with the document link in the description and an EMAIL reminder 1 minute
   before the event.
Also: do a dry run for today RIGHT NOW so I can check your work.

    Google Calendar sends email reminders natively. Donna sets one on the event, and at 4:54 PM the email lands with your briefing link inside.

    On Outlook, the 4:54 PM nudge arrives as a calendar notification on your laptop and phone, with the briefing link one tap away. Microsoft’s calendar API doesn’t yet let apps attach email reminders to events; Outlook keeps that switch inside its own interface.

    Want a true email at 4:54 in Outlook too?

    Per event, by hand: open the event in Outlook on the web → Edit → More options → the Remind me dropdown → + Add email reminder. Works on your own calendar in Outlook on the web and the new Outlook (not classic desktop).

    Once, forever: a five-minute Power Automate flow: trigger “When an upcoming event is starting soon” → action “Send an email.” Set it once and every “Briefing with Donna” event emails you automatically. Grab Denise after the workshop and she’ll set it up with you.

  3. Watch the dry run

    That last line is the whole show: Donna does today’s run right now. Give her a minute, then check: the briefing doc is in Donna Briefings, the 📋 Briefing with Donna event is on today’s calendar, and the drafts from Step 3 are waiting in your email.

The trick

Claude never emails you. Your calendar does. The briefing lands at 4:54 PM from a sender you already trust. No new app to check, no new noise: just one appointment, every day, with everything that matters attached.

Advanced: Donna runs both calendars

Juggling a work Microsoft 365 calendar, a personal Google one, and a board calendar or two? Connect both calendar connectors (Step 1 showed you how), then add a morning scheduled task: Donna reads every calendar, folds the unified day, conflicts included, into your briefing, and, if you like, writes “busy” holds onto your primary calendar mirroring the others.

The insight: no calendar ever needs access to another. Donna is the only one who sees them all, and she writes into one.
